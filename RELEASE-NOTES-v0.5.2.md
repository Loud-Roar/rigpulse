# RigPulse v0.5.2

- Rebuilds Nerd Console around the requested information hierarchy: pool status, miner cards, wallet balances, fleet best shares, blocks found, and network difficulty.
- Moves configured SoloPool accounts and wallet values to the top of the Nerd Console dashboard, followed immediately by miner status cards.
- Adds live online-worker gauge rings to BTC and BCH SoloPool panels.
- Replaces the generic blue-glass treatment with pale instrument panels, black display zones, lime/yellow hashrate readouts, magenta accents, and an original vector world-grid background.
- Adds icon-led temperature, power, share, efficiency, uptime, pool, and fan cells to local miner cards.
- Makes network difficulty substantially more prominent in the BTC and BCH cards.
- Leaves every non-Nerd theme and the standard dashboard ordering unchanged.
