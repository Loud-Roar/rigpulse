# RigPulse v0.4.0

- Detects miner-reported found blocks and stores the winning miner state.
- Adds a full-screen BLOCK FOUND party, ten-second miner-card confetti, and replay control.
- Keeps a BLOCK FOUND badge until the miner counter resets or its pool changes.
- Shows BTC and BCH network strips side by side.
- Adds current-share difficulty beside best share on miner cards.
- Adds optional read-only BTC and BCH public-address balances.
