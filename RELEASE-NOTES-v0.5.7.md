# RigPulse v0.5.7

This corrective release replaces the legacy tiled miner-card structure with the
approved integrated RigPulse instrument layout.

## Changes

- Rebuilds every local miner card around a unified instrument face.
- Places the miner identity and status in a dedicated RigPulse header.
- Adds a central fan and chip-temperature rail with live gauges.
- Groups power, efficiency, lifetime shares, and reject rate as electrical and
  operating readings.
- Places a large, high-contrast Best Share reading directly beside Live
  Hashrate.
- Keeps Current Share Difficulty visible in the miner-status area.
- Integrates the one-hour hashrate graph into the instrument face.
- Reflows the layout for narrow mobile screens while keeping Best Share and
  Live Hashrate together.
